# Green Park — business planning and funding readiness

**Engagement:** September 2026. **My role:** Lead Consultant, Kuziere LMS.

I developed the business-planning and funding-readiness package for a proposed Nigerian residential development with 50 terrace homes and a retained neighbourhood retail component. The revised V6 package was submitted on 25 September 2026. This case documents completed consulting deliverables; development completion, funding approval and forecast returns are not claimed as achieved outcomes.

## The business question

Could the proposed sales and delivery program fund land acquisition, construction and financing obligations without relying on unsupported assumptions? A profitable project can still run short of cash when customer instalments arrive later than land payments, construction bills or debt repayments.

The assignment therefore required one integrated factual and financial base, with separate presentations for management, prospective investors and lenders.

## Method and decision process

1. **Clarify the source material.** Review the sponsor's initial cash-flow and development materials, issue a clarification questionnaire, and follow up on funding, collection and delivery timing.
2. **Separate assumptions by evidence status.** Distinguish management-confirmed inputs from provisional modelling choices and items requiring professional validation. For example, the model's tax basis required adviser confirmation; unconfirmed retail income remained outside the residential base case.
3. **Model the monthly sequence.** Connect contract cohorts, instalment dates and handovers to construction spending, equity contributions, debt draws, supplier support and repayments.
4. **Test profit and liquidity separately.** Trace revenue through costs, finance charges and tax, while checking when cash shortages arise and which temporary funding source bridges each gap.
5. **Make the recommendation conditional.** Use explicit gates for approvals, funding documentation, market evidence, cost and program, tax/accounting and retail assumptions.
6. **Reconcile the audience-specific documents.** Use the same V6 case for the plan, executive summary and investor/lender materials; provide a compact creditor bridge for reviewing principal, interest and cash.

## A concrete modelling decision

Following sponsor feedback, buyer payments were revised to **20:15:15:20:20:10**, with the last 10% due at handover. Earlier instalments falling beyond a buyer's delivery date were accelerated before handover. Possession required the full contract price to have cleared.

First handovers were modelled for October 2027; final handovers and collections aligned in September 2028. The revised V6 submission reported peak supplier support of approximately **NGN 660.5 million**. This is a modelled requirement under the stated collection schedule, not evidence that a supplier facility was approved or drawn.

A separate review identified that scheduled senior repayments covered **NGN 1.4 billion principal**, while **NGN 443.7 million capitalised interest** remained outstanding at forecast close. Principal repayment alone therefore could not be described as full debt settlement. The financing documents and final cash waterfall needed an explicit interest-settlement route.

## Result and professional relevance

The completed outcome was a reconciled planning and funding-discussion package with assumptions, liquidity requirements and unresolved financing conditions made explicit. It supported a **conditional proceed** position for preparation and controlled pre-sales, subject to the decision gates.

This demonstrates financial modelling, requirements clarification, document quality review, executive synthesis and evidence-based judgment. Those methods transfer to evaluating AI-generated business documents: check whether figures reconcile, assumptions are supported, cash and profit are distinguished, and recommendations respect unresolved conditions.

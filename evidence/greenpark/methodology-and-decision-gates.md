## Collection and accounting logic

| Payment event | Share | Model treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sale | 20% | Cash receipt; customer advance until handover |
| After one month | 15% | Cash collection |
| After three months | 15% | Cash collection |
| After six months | 20% | Cash collection |
| After twelve months or earlier before delivery | 20% | Accelerate where needed to fall before handover |
| Handover | 10% | Final receipt; full price must clear before possession |

The model distinguishes contracted sales, cash collected, customer advances and handover-based revenue. A reservation or signed contract does not substitute for cash available to fund construction.

## Six decision gates

| Gate | Evidence required | Action in the delivered plan |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Land and approvals | Title/layout/approval status statement and supporting documents | Confirm before external circulation |
| Funding | Executed debt and deferral terms, including interest settlement | Confirm before major commitments |
| Market | Comparable pricing, broker pipeline and buyer interviews | Complete before full sales launch |
| Cost and program | BOQ, procurement plan, milestone program and cost to complete | Approve before full mobilisation |
| Tax and accounting | Project-specific tax advice and accounting treatment | Update model before external release |
| Retail component | Lettable area, rent, occupancy, operating costs and cap rate | Keep upside excluded until validated |

## Operating controls

- Reconcile each buyer account to amounts due and cleared receipts before handover.
- Maintain one inventory and collections register for reservations, contracts, discounts, cancellations and allocations.
- Review pipeline and collections weekly; report inventory, due amounts, receipts and overdue balances monthly.
- Apply customer collections to certified project costs and agreed financing obligations through a controlled project account.
- Require approval for discounts, price changes and cost shocks passed into unit pricing.
- Confirm facility obligations, tax, completion costs and retentions before distributions.
